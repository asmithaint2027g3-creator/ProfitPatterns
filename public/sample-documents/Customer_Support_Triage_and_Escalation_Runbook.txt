WORKFLOW TITLE: Customer Support Ticket Triage, SLA Priority Tagging & Escalation
BUSINESS UNIT: Global Customer Operations & Success
APPLICATION: ProfitPatterns AI Diagnostic Intake Sample

1. CONTEXT:
The support operations team receives 1,400+ weekly customer requests across web forms, 
email, and chat. Agents currently spend the first 2 hours of every shift manually reading, 
tagging, assigning urgency tiers, and pinging engineering leads for technical blockers.

2. CURRENT PROCESS STEPS:
1. Agent opens Zendesk Unassigned Queue.
2. Reads inquiry text and customer account tier (Enterprise, Mid-Market, Standard).
3. Evaluates sentiment and urgency (e.g., system downtime vs password reset).
4. Assigns 4 tags: [Product_Area], [Issue_Type], [SLA_Tier], [Priority_Level].
5. For technical bugs, manually duplicates ticket details into Jira and messages on-call engineer on Slack.
6. For standard questions, copies canned response macros from Google Docs.

3. MEASURED INEFFICIENCIES:
- Median time to first human triage: 47 minutes during peak business hours.
- 12% of high-severity enterprise tickets are initially tagged with normal priority.
- Agents spend ~28 hours per week collectively performing repetitive categorization.

4. OBJECTIVES FOR PROFITPATTERNS AUDIT:
- Feasibility study for deploying an autonomous AI Triage Agent to classify incoming tickets in < 30 seconds.
- Auto-draft contextual resolution replies for top 10 recurring issues with human review.
- Projected payback timeframe: 3 months with 40% reduction in median first-response time.
