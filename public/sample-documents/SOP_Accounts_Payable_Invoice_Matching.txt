TITLE: Vendor Invoice Verification, Discrepancy Matching & ERP Entry Workflow
DEPARTMENT: Finance & Accounts Payable
PROCESS OWNER: Lead Financial Controller
TARGET FOR AI AUDIT: Process Automation & Human-in-the-Loop LLM Extraction

1. PROCESS OBJECTIVE:
The purpose of this procedure is to verify incoming vendor PDF invoices, cross-reference 
line-item charges against approved Purchase Orders (POs) and Delivery Receipts, 
resolve pricing discrepancies, and manually key line-item records into NetSuite/QuickBooks.

2. CURRENT RESOURCE CONSUMPTION & METRICS:
- AP Specialists Involved: 3 full-time equivalents (FTEs)
- Weekly Manual Time Spent: ~32–38 hours per week total
- Monthly Invoice Volume: 850 – 1,200 PDF invoices
- Average Processing Time per Invoice: 11.5 minutes
- Human Error / Discrepancy Rate: 4.8% (data transcription typos, tax code mismatch)
- Estimated Annual Operational Cost: $78,000 USD in manual labor

3. STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW:

Step 1: Ingestion & Sorting
- Staff checks shared inbox (invoices@company.com).
- Download PDF invoices, verify they are not duplicates, and save to Google Drive / SharePoint.
- Open Excel tracking sheet "2026_AP_Batch_Register.xlsx".

Step 2: Line-Item Cross-Check
- Open PO in NetSuite to verify vendor name, PO number, line items, unit quantities, and prices.
- Open shipping bill/delivery receipt to confirm inventory received.
- Compare vendor line prices against agreed contract rates.

Step 3: Discrepancy Handling
- If price variance > $15 or missing items, specialist flags the invoice.
- Specialist drafts an email to vendor requesting credit memo or revised invoice.
- Invoice placed in pending hold folder.

Step 4: Manual ERP Entry & GL Coding
- Manually key in: Vendor ID, Invoice Date, Due Date, Tax Amounts, GL Account Codes, 
  and Department Cost Centers into the accounting software.
- Route for manager approval if invoice exceeds $5,000 threshold.

4. PRIMARY BOTTLENECK & PAIN POINTS:
- Inconsistent vendor PDF layouts (multi-page tables, scanned handwriting, varied date formats).
- High cognitive fatigue from copying & pasting item codes between three separate browser tabs.
- Month-end reconciliation crunch leading to 2-3 days delay in financial closing.

5. DESIRED AI & AUTOMATION AUDIT OUTCOME:
- Implement automated document extraction (OCR + Document LLM) capable of parsing 
  unstructured PDF invoices with 98%+ accuracy.
- Automated two-way matching against NetSuite POs with confidence scoring.
- Reduce manual verification time from 11.5 minutes to under 1 minute per invoice.
- Projected target annual savings: ~$55,000+ and 80% reduction in month-end overtime.
